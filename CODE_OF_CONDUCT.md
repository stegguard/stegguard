# Code of Conduct

StegGuard contributors must make project spaces safe, respectful, and useful.
This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release work, and
other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Protect private security reports and personal information.
- Accept correction, repair harm, and move the work forward.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers listed in
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). If the concern involves a listed maintainer,
contact another maintainer and exclude the involved person. Reports will be
handled as privately as practical.

Maintainers may warn a participant, remove content, restrict participation, or
ban someone based on severity and repeated behavior. Retaliation against a
reporter or witness is itself a violation. Maintainers must disclose conflicts
and recuse themselves when appropriate.
