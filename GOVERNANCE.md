# Governance

StegGuard uses a maintainer-led, evidence-first model. Contributors propose and
review changes in public issues and pull requests. Maintainers are accountable
for releases, security response, compatibility, and repository policy.

## Decisions

Routine changes are accepted after required checks pass and a maintainer
approves. Public API changes, report-schema changes, detector-policy changes,
and governance changes need a written rationale and at least one maintainer who
did not author the change. Maintainers seek consensus; if consensus is not
possible, the project lead records the decision and tradeoffs in the issue or
pull request.

Security response may happen privately until coordinated disclosure. The final
advisory and compatibility impact must be made public.

## Maintainers

Maintainers are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). A contributor may be
invited after sustained, technically sound participation and demonstrated care
with reviews and private information. Removal may follow inactivity, request,
or a documented conduct or trust concern. Adding or removing a maintainer is a
governance change and must be recorded publicly unless safety requires limited
details.

## Ownership

Until a verified GitHub team or handle is available for review routing, this
repository intentionally does not claim an effective `CODEOWNERS` rule. The
maintainer list is the human source of truth. Add `CODEOWNERS` only together
with verified accounts and branch rules that require its reviews.
